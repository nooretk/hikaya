## sebastia - "What can I see from the Roman period?"

- **Answered from reviewed source:** yes
- **Backend:** `offline-extractive`

**Answer:**

The Roman theatre - on the eastern side of the city, built in the Roman period for performances, celebrations and public assemblies; semi-circular with stone seating. The royal tombs - rock-cut tombs; archaeologists attribute some to the ancient Israelite period and others to the Roman and Byzantine periods. They have stone entrances, burial chambers and areas for funerary rites. Cathedral of St John / Mosque of Nabi Yahya - built in the Byzantine period, rebuilt by the Crusaders, and partly converted into the Mosque of Nabi Yahya in the Islamic period.
